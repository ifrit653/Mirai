# Blue Bill

**A narrative point-and-click game set in Madagascar, where corruption is a daily struggle.**

You play as a police officer facing moral and ethical dilemmas every day. Each case presents a new offender, a new story, and a difficult decision: **arrest** or **release**. But the truth isn’t always clear.

---

## 📖 About the Game

Blue Bill is inspired by _Papers, Please_ and explores themes of corruption, temptation, and the human cost of poverty and power. Your choices shape your story and the world around you.

- **Interact with your surroundings** and **inspect evidence** like a detective.
- **Question suspects** and uncover the truth behind each case.
- **Make tough decisions**—will you uphold justice or succumb to bribes?

---

## 🎮 Gameplay Features

- **Branching Narrative:** Your choices have real consequences.
- **Moral Dilemmas:** Every decision affects your reputation and the story’s outcome.
- **Rich Storytelling:** Uncover the personal stories behind each case.
- **Authentic Setting:** Experience the challenges of law enforcement in a corrupt system.
